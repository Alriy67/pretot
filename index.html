chapter three · what i wanted to say

a letter, from me
to you.

hello, pixie :3

wala nang pasikot-sikot pa bwhahahaha, direct to the point na agad 😛 i miss you.

honestly, hindi ko alam kung bakit sa’yo lang ako naging ganito. ewan ko ba kung ginayuma mo ako o ano, pero kahit anong gawin ko, ikaw at ikaw pa rin talaga. i miss you more than i can explain, and i think i’ve missed almost everything about you: your presence, your smile, our conversations, our little moments, and even the ordinary things we used to do together.

i hope you’re doing okay over there, lalo na kapag may period ka ehe. pero kidding aside, i genuinely hope you’re taking care of yourself. i hope you’re happy, and i hope life has been treating you well. and of course, i hope you miss me too :^[

i’m writing this because there’s something i’ve wanted to ask you for a while now. pixie, may i court you again? can you give me another chance to show you how much i truly love you?

i know this might sound a little unexpected, pero gusto ko nang maging honest sa nararamdaman ko. mahirap para sa akin na itago pa, especially when talking to you still makes me happy in ways i can’t explain. kahit simpleng conversation lang natin, kahit random stories or pangungumusta lang, somehow, you still manage to make my day better. sometimes, i find myself smiling at my phone because of you, and honestly, nakakahiya na rin minsan bwhahahaha.

one of the things i appreciate most about you is how you still show concern for me. you might not realize it, but those little things mean so much to me. every time you ask if i’m okay or show that you care, it reminds me why i fell for you in the first place. and somehow, it makes me fall for you even more.

i still remember how shy you used to be whenever someone teased us. bwhahahaha, natatandaan ko pa kung paano bumibilis ang lakad mo kapag may nang-aasar sa atin. those little moments may seem ordinary, but they’re memories i’ll always cherish. whenever i remember them, i can’t help but smile because they remind me of the time when everything between us felt so simple and happy.

i miss those moments, pixie. i miss laughing with you, talking to you about random things, and sharing little pieces of our days. i miss the comfort of having you around and the happiness that came with even the smallest interactions between us. more than anything, i miss you—not just the memories we made, but you as a person. i miss your personality, your little habits, your reactions, and all the little things that make you who you are.

sometimes, i wonder if you still remember those moments the way i do. i wonder if there are times when something reminds you of us and makes you smile, even just for a second. because for me, there are still moments when a random memory of you crosses my mind, and suddenly, i’m smiling to myself like an idiot. bwhahahaha, look what you’ve done to me, pixie. >:(

i know things may not be exactly the same as they used to be, and i’m not expecting everything to go back to how it was overnight. but if you give me another chance, i want to make new memories with you. i want to know you even more, listen to your stories, learn about the things that make you happy, and be there for the little moments that matter to you.

i don’t want my feelings to remain just words. i want to show you through my actions how much you mean to me. i want to keep learning, growing, and becoming a better version of myself. i know i have my flaws, and i know there may be things i still need to improve, but my feelings for you are genuine. i’m willing to put effort into showing you that, not just by saying i love you, but by making sure my actions reflect it, too.

and honestly, that’s one of the reasons i made this website for you. :3 i wanted to create something special, something that could express the feelings i sometimes struggle to put into words. every little detail is here because it reminds me of you or of the memories we’ve shared. i hope that when you look through it, you’ll smile, even just a little. i hope you’ll feel how much thought and effort i put into making it for you.

bwhahahaha, imagine making a whole website just to confess your feelings. who would’ve thought i’d actually do something like this? 😭 but i guess some people are just worth the effort, and for me, you’re one of them.

pixie, i still love you. and i don’t want to say that just because i miss you or because talking to you makes me happy. i want you to know that my feelings are something i’ve thought about, something i’ve come to understand, and something i genuinely want to express to you.

i don’t know exactly what the future holds for us, but i know what i want to ask you today. i want the chance to court you again, to show you my intentions, and to prove through my actions that my feelings are real.

i miss you so much, pixie. as in sobra bwhahahaha, nakakahiya na ’to pero sige na nga, nandito na tayo, eh. might as well say everything i’ve been keeping to myself.

iloveyou, pixie. :3 and i really, really hope you know how much you mean to me.
